1
Marching Vessels
ALL
Tank

A line of seven Blood Creations spawns on one side of the arena and sends waves across the platform. Real waves are lethal or apply a heavy DoT. With True Sight, safe illusion lanes become visible.

Whoever has True Sight should call the pattern on voice. Common calls (lanes numbered 1–7 from left to right when facing the wave):

  • RIGHT Safe lanes on the right side (lanes 6–7).
  • LEFT Safe lanes on the left side (lanes 1–2).
  • MIDDLE Safe center lanes (lanes 3–4–5).
  • OUTSIDE / ONE Safe odd lanes 1–3–5–7.
  • INSIDE / TWO Safe even lanes 2–4–6.
Timing
First wave spawns at 6s · every 15s after that
Tips
You can skip waves with a portal or certain mobility abilities (for example Xavian's Decree of the Sun and Sky Crash).
2
Truesight Corruption
ALL
Tank

Click a corner Blood Altar for 20s Truesight Corruption. You see safe lanes. A dark circle lets allies dip in for short True Sight (5s) — the circle ticks damage, so the tank usually holds the buff.

  • HARD+ Tired Eyes — stacks every ~2s while Corruption is up (+1% damage taken per stack); ~15s residual after it falls.
  • Cannot refresh while active. Lost after One True Path — take True Sight from an altar again after the maze.
Timing
On demand · duration exactly 20s
Tips
If Tired Eyes gets too high, skip a wave set without Truesight Corruption (portal the wave, or let a non-tank take the altar once).
3
Blood Orbs
NIGHTMARE
DPS · Tank kite

An orb spawns from the last altar used for True Sight and crawls toward the boss. Contact heals the boss (~5% HP). Orbs can be slowed, CCed, and damaged; 35% of damage dealt to the orb transfers to the boss.

  • Orbs persist into One True Path and keep moving while you cannot freely attack.
  • You can control where the pre-maze orb spawns by choosing which altar was used last — see T2.
  • During the final burn phase, orbs spawn from random locations — not from the last altar.
Timing
First 15s · every 27s
Tips
Remember (or mark) the last altar you used so you know where the next orb will come from. If an orb blocks your path, stun it and walk the boss past — he will not soak a stunned orb.
4
Hungering Vessels
ALL
Kick / CC

Two vessels spawn and channel Gorging Pool (~12s): party-wide ticks (~2s) and a large slow zone. Interrupt or stun immediately — long channels wipe.

  • Two spawns per cycle, ~1.5s apart.
Timing
First ~20s · every ~40s
Tips
Pre-assign a kick / CC rotation so both vessels die quickly.
5
Tormenting Visions
ALL
Tank

5s channel that pulses heavy damage around the tank. Melee stacked on the tank can die instantly.

  • The cast requires True Sight on the tank. You can interrupt the cast by letting True Sight drop mid-channel; leftover cast time is added to the cooldown. See T1.
  • If you hold True Sight for a long time (or skip it), the boss waits and will only start Tormenting Visions once TS is up again.
  • NIGHTMARE Keep the boss away from Blood Orbs and from corners where the next orb will spawn. During the 5s channel the boss stands still and can easily soak an orb. If your Tormenting Visions timer is desynced, be careful the next time you take True Sight from an altar: the cast can start immediately and root him on top of an orb.
Timing
First at 25s · 25s cooldown from cast start (5s channel + remaining CD)
Tips
Clear melee before the pulse window.
6
Eradication
ALL
Defensive · Kick / CC

Sinister Vessels on four sides each aim a beam at one player. Beam cast is exactly 5s. Your own beam is survivable; another player's beam is lethal. Spread lines. Vessels can be kicked or CCed to stop the cast early.

  • HARD+ Lingering Eradication — your own beam applies an 8s magic DoT (dispellable).
Timing
First at 26s · every 25s · beam cast 5s
Tips
During telegraph, beams track — leave foreign lines before the snap. DPS who struggle to live their own beam can run 10 green gems so their defensive is available for every Eradication cast.
7
Contrite
ALL
Tank

Tank slam applying Contrite Weakness (~6s): later hits deal stacking +20% damage. Respect stacks before eating your Eradication beam.

Timing
Recurring tank slam
Tips
Avoid overlapping high stacks with Eradication / Tormenting Visions without a defensive.
8
One True Path
ALLPHASE
Tank

At 70% and 50% HP, regular mechanics cancel. Each player gets a unique path (30s) with temporary True Sight. Touching another player outside safe zones is lethal. Abilities unlock in the golden dome.

The transition can be delayed if the boss is channeling Tormenting Visions at the HP threshold.

  • Waves still cross the maze — yield and wait.
Timing
Boss HP 70% and 50% (may delay if Tormenting Visions is channeling)
Tips
  1. Agree the exit order from the start bubble before the phase.
  2. Bloodrite Drums speed up the maze: use them just before the phase, or inside the dome after ~2s. Pressing them the instant the phase starts puts them on CD with no effect.
  3. You can use a portal if you still reach the start portal and the exit portal sits on your path.
  4. A maze bug can wipe the group — it is controllable. See T3.
9
Repent
ALLPHASE
Dispel

If the maze is failed, the party gets a heavy magic DoT (~20s). Mass Dispel is the emergency plan — prevention is better.

While Repent is up you cannot take True Sight. The only reliable way through Marching Vessels is a portal.

Timing
On failed One True Path
10
Blood Sermon
ALLPHASE
Tank

At 25% HP, three runes appear (Cross / Sword / Eye). True Sight shows the correct one. Place the required number of players before the cast ends to grant party True Sight for burn.

The transition can be delayed if the boss is channeling Tormenting Visions when he hits 25%.

  • Cross — all four · Sword — exactly one · Eye — one with Truesight Corruption
Tips
Plan your True Sight window ahead of 25%. If Blood Sermon starts while your TS is about to expire, ask a teammate to take the altar so someone can still call the correct rune.
11
Apotheosis
ALLPHASE
Party

Throughout the final phase, random ground explosions fill the arena. Dodge cracks/puddles while still handling waves, pools, eradication, and (on Nightmare) orbs.

Tips

T1
Interrupt Tormenting Visions with True Sight
Tank

Tormenting Visions only channels meaningfully while you have True Sight. If you pick up the altar so that True Sight has about 1 second left when the Tormenting Visions cast starts, the channel is interrupted after that second instead of running the full 5s.

A practical rule: take True Sight 19 seconds before the next Tormenting Visions window. TS lasts 20s, so it drops almost immediately after Tormenting Visions begins — the cast is interrupted early.

  • Less pulse damage on you and anyone near the boss.
  • More free movement time to kite the boss away from Blood Orbs.
Tips
If you use a portal, your TS timer will desync.
T2
Control orb spawns into the maze
Tank · Nightmare

During One True Path the boss stays parked in the same corner he started the fight in. Blood Orbs always spawn from the last altar someone used for True Sight and then walk toward him.

If the last altar before the maze is the one farthest from the boss corner, a late orb has a long path. Spawn it just before the intermission and the boss often will not soak it while everyone is locked in the maze.

  • At pull, mark the boss's starting corner so the whole group always knows which altar is "far".
  • Plan the last pre-maze True Sight from that far altar on purpose.
T3
Maze safe-zone timing bug
Party

There is a known One True Path bug: if the last player enters the safe dome while a new wave set is still spawning (statues appearing / wave not yet moving), the entire group can die.

You can play around it. When you are the last person approaching the dome, wait until the wave has actually started moving, then step in. Do not finish the maze on the spawn tick.
